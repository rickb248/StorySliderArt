# Meg — Level 4 production master

**Canon scope:** Meg is the active Level 4 recurring human anchor in *Meg in a Box*, *Max and Meg at the Lab*, and *In the Fog*. The approved prose establishes only those specific events; it does not establish a home, ownership, or relationship beyond their written roles.

**Production visual design:** Approximately seven years old; medium warm-brown skin; black hair in two neat puff buns with dusty-lavender ties; large dark-brown anime eyes; muted teal cardigan over a warm cream T-shirt; dusty-plum skirt-over-leggings; soft gray-lavender sneakers. Curious, calm, reassuring expression. Rounded young K–2 proportions.
