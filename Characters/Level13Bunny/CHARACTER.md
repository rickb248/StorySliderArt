# Level 13 wild bunny — story-local visual record

**Canonical scope:** The unnamed wild bunny appears in *The Little Bunny*. It retreats to a hedge after a twig falls, later returns, sits in the sun, and eats a green leaf. Do not treat it as a pet or infer ownership, name, sex, or future appearances.

**Non-canonical visual production choice:** Small natural cottontail bunny with warm gray-brown fur, pale cream belly, white tail, upright ears with muted pink inner fur, and dark gentle eyes; no clothing.
