# Level 13 goat — story-local visual record

**Canonical scope:** The unnamed goat exists only inside Jo and June's invented story in *The Silly Story*. The goat calls the pig's wig a saddle and carries the pig to a party. Do not establish it outside the story-within-a-story.

**Non-canonical visual production choice:** Small natural cream-and-warm-gray goat with short curved horns, darker ears and lower legs, black hooves, small beard, and friendly expressive eyes; no clothing.
