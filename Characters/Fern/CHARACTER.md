# Fern — Level 15 visual record

**Canonical scope:** Fern uses she/her pronouns and has a mother and father. Her toy train, letter, rug, sled, and play with Kirk are event-bound; do not treat them as permanent traits.

**Non-canonical visual production choice:** A rounded 6–8-year-old girl with deep brown skin, soft coily black hair in two puff buns, large dark-brown eyes, a muted dusty-blue pinafore over a cream long-sleeve shirt, plum leggings, and soft gray-lilac sneakers.
