# Character Description: Ben's Pug

**Canon scope:** Ben says "My pug" in *The Pug at the Vet*; it is his pug and reappears in
*The Pug and the Rag*. This pug is not Pip the pig and must never be substituted for Pip.

**Non-canonical visual design for production:** A small natural fawn pug with a black muzzle,
folded ears, curled tail, and large gentle anime-style eyes. It wears no clothes and stays on
four legs except when naturally seated.
