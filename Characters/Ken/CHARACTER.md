# Character Description: Ken

**Canon scope:** Ken appears only in Level 3 R4, *The Tub Jet*. He owns a red tub, places it on a rug, and imagines that he is in a jet. The tub remains physically grounded; no flight, family, home, or other durable fact is established.

**Non-canonical visual production design:** A six-year-old Black boy with deep brown skin, short springy black curls, a rust-orange T-shirt, navy shorts, and white-and-orange sneakers. He has large warm anime-style eyes and compact K–2 proportions.
