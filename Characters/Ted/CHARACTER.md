# Character Description: Ted

**Canon scope:** Ted appears only in Level 3, *The Hum and the Hop*. The source calls him
Ted and shows him hopping; it establishes no family, home, or durable relationship.

**Non-canonical visual design for production:** A six-year-old boy with medium golden-brown
skin, close-cropped dark curls, a teal T-shirt, charcoal shorts, and white sneakers.
He has large calm anime-style eyes and a kind expression. This design is a visual aid only.
