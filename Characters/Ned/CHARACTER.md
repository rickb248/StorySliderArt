# Character Description: Ned

**Canon scope:** Ned calls his father Dad in Level 3, *Ned at the Fun Run*. This establishes
Ned's father only; it does not establish a shared identity with Ben's father.

**Non-canonical visual design for production:** A six-year-old boy with light olive skin,
straight black hair in a tidy short cut, a red zip hoodie, navy shorts, and white running
sneakers. Large friendly anime-style eyes; cheerful, ready-to-run posture.
