# Character Description: Ben's Dad

**Canon scope:** Ben calls this adult Dad in *The Big Red Hat*. He is Ben's father. Do not
merge him with Ned's father or the generic Dad reference.

**Non-canonical visual design for production:** A friendly middle-aged man with deep brown
skin, short neat black hair, a forest-green cardigan over a cream shirt, dark blue trousers,
and brown loafers. Warm anime-style eyes and a gentle smile.
