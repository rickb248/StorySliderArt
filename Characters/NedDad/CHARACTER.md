# Character Description: Ned's Dad

**Canon scope:** Ned calls this adult Dad in *Ned at the Fun Run*. He is Ned's father. Do
not merge him with Ben's father or the generic Dad reference.

**Non-canonical visual design for production:** A friendly middle-aged man with fair skin,
wavy auburn hair, a cobalt-blue polo shirt, tan chinos, and grey running shoes. He has warm
anime-style eyes and an encouraging expression.
