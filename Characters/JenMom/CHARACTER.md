# Character Description: Jen's Mom

**Canon scope:** Jen runs to an adult called Mom in Level 3 R4, *The Red Bag*, and Mom lets Jen have a red bag. She is Jen's caregiver. Do not merge her with another child’s Mom or infer any additional family, home, or ownership facts.

**Non-canonical visual production design:** A friendly middle-aged Black woman with deep umber skin, shoulder-length soft dark curls, a dusty teal cardigan over a cream shirt, plum trousers, and brown flats. She has warm anime-style eyes and a gentle expression.
