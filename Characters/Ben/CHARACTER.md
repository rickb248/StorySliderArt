# Character Description: Ben

**Age Group:** 6 years old (K-2 Appropriate).
**Appearance:**
*   **Skin Tone:** Fair porcelain skin tone.
*   **Hair:** Short, messy ginger/red hair.
*   **Facial Features:** Large expressive anime-style eyes, friendly simple features, small smile.
*   **Style:** Modern Storybook Anime style with clear black outlines.
*   **Clothing:** 
    *   Yellow t-shirt.
    *   Blue jeans.
    *   Brown shoes.
**Personality:** Warm-hearted, sharing, friendly.
