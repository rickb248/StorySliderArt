# Level 8 Shared ImageGen Prompt

Applied to every accepted Level 8 master, with story-specific directives appended:

> Create a square children's reading-app card illustration in the established StorySlider series style: polished hand-painted 2D digital illustration, softly textured shapes, warm restrained outlines, calm cohesive muted palette, natural anatomy, expressive but gentle faces. PURE WHITE BACKGROUND visibly connected to all four edges. No scenery, no room, no environmental frame, no border. One tight centered focal cluster at accepted StorySlider level-card subject scale, generous white margins, only the smallest necessary semantic props or cues. No text, letters, numbers, captions, signs, labels, logos, watermarks, UI, or speech bubbles. Do not anthropomorphize ordinary objects.

Operational history:

- Generator: built-in ImageGen for every generation and image edit.
- Accepted masters were resized once to exactly 600×600 with ffmpeg; resizing did not alter semantic content.
- Rejected generations were never installed as masters.
- Character reference paths and accepted-generation notes are recorded in each story's `PROMPTS.md`.
